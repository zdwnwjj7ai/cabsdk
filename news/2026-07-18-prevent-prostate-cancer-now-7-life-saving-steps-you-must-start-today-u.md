Prostate cancer prevention involves making healthy lifestyle choices. Eating a balanced diet, rich in fruits and vegetables, can be beneficial. Regular physical activity helps maintain a healthy weight, which is important since obesity is linked to a higher risk of prostate cancer. Avoiding smoking and limiting alcohol intake are also recommended.

Key Prevention Tips:

Healthy Diet: Focus on eating a variety of fruits, vegetables, and whole grains. Try to limit red meat and high-fat dairy products.
