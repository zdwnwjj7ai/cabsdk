Can stress alone mess up my hormones?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, stress directly affects your hormones. When you're stressed, your body releases cortisol and adrenaline, which can disrupt other key hormones like estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, thyroid hormones, and insulin. This hormonal imbalance often leads to noticeable changes in mood, energy, sleep, weight, and menstrual cycles.

Because stress-related hormone shifts can mimic or overlap with other medical conditions—such as thyroid disorders, PCOS, or adrenal issues—it's important to identify what's actually driving your symptoms.
