Who Is Dave Palumbo?

Before we talk about Palumboism (bubble gut), it helps to know the man whose name became attached to it.

Dave Palumbo is a longtime, highly recognizable figure in bodybuilding. A former competitive bodybuilder turned media powerhouse, he’s best known today as the founder of RX Muscle, where he hosts podcasts, interviews top athletes, and breaks down everything from training to PED use. Even after stepping away from the stage, he’s remained deeply embedded in the culture of the sport.

Palumbo didn’t start out chasing mass. He was originally a long-distance runner before shifting his focus to weights. That shift paid off quickly. By the mid-1990s, he had packed on serious size, winning the NPC Junior Nationals overall in 1995 and later placing second in the Super Heavyweight division at the NPC USA Championships in 2003. At his peak, he was known for extreme thickness and sheer muscle density.

Over time, however, fans noticed something else: a dramatically distended midsection that didn’t match the rest of his physique. It wasn’t just aging or offseason weight-it looked different, exaggerated, and permanent. As similar physiques began appearing across the sport, bodybuilding forums and fans coined the term “Palumboism” to describe the bubble-gut look that came to define an entire era of extreme mass building.
