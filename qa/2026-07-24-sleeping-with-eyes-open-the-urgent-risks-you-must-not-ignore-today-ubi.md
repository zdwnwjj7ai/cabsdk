Is it possible to fall asleep with your eyes open, and what does it indicate?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, it is possible to fall asleep with your eyes open. This condition, known as nocturnal lagophthalmos, occurs when the eyelids do not close completely during sleep. It can result from a variety of causes, including facial nerve damage, eyelid abnormalities, thyroid issues, or certain neurological conditions. People with nocturnal lagophthalmos often experience dry, irritated eyes, blurred vision upon waking, or a gritty sensation in the eyes.

Because sleeping with your eyes open can point to underlying nerve, muscle, or eye health issues, it's important not to ignore persistent symptoms.
