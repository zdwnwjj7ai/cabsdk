People usually think of acne as a teenage problem, but the truth is that it affects men of all ages. Fortunately, you can banish breakouts with these 3 simple tips.

Blast bacteria There are tons of products that’ll remove pore-clogging bacteria from your face (we’ll address those below). But avoiding it to begin with is your first step, especially since your cheeks are your problem spot. Think about it: Your cell phone is covered in who-knows-what (maybe even fecal matter), your pillowcase has residue from your hair products, and you probably touch your face throughout the day. You’re not alone, but these little things add up—and clog your pores. Talk on speakerphone when possible and change your pillowcase at least once a week, even if you don’t change your sheets that often.

[Can Reduce](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/6146825299.html)

Watch your diet A diet high in carbohydrates and dairy could cause breakouts. Researchers believe that carbs increase insulin, which causes inflammation in the skin and leads to breakouts. The hormones in dairy increase sebum production—an oily substance that can clog pores, as we reported in Breakaway from Breakouts.

Use the right products Look for products with salicylic acid and benzoyl peroxide, the two most common acne medications. Salicylic acid is a beta hydroxy acid that removes dead skin from the pores and reduces inflammation, while benzoyl peroxide kills bacteria and reduces redness. Try an oil-free cleanser with 2 percent salicylic acid—like Neutrogena's Oil Free Acne Wash. Since benzoyl peroxide can cause dryness, use it as a spot treatment when needed. We suggest Dermalogica’s Medibac Clearing Booster. And don’t forget to use an oil-free moisturizer daily. Your body will produce pore-clogging oils to compensate for dry skin.
