What drinks offer the best hangover relief?

Some research indicates that many hangover symptoms are caused or exacerbated by dehydration, so one of the best things you can do to fight a hangover is to focus on rehydrating yourself. Next, we will review some of the best drinks for a hangover.

Caffeine-free teas

Teas not containing caffeine are mostly made up of water, so they can help you increase your water intake when you are hungover. Some teas (such as green tea and honey chrysanthemum tea) have been shown to possibly prevent liver damage and help the body process alcohol more quickly.

[Hangover Relief](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/)

It is important to avoid caffeinated teas during a hangover because caffeine is a diuretic that can worsen dehydration.

Electrolyte drinks

Electrolytes are minerals that are essential for your body’s normal function that are found in your body’s fluids.

Broths and sports drinks are high in electrolytes, which get out of balance when dehydrated. Electrolyte drinks will hydrate you and restore a healthy electrolyte balance.

Fruit, herb, mineral, vegetable, and vitamin drinks

Drinking juices, smoothies, and other beverages that contain these ingredients can also relieve hangover symptoms.

As a specific example, a beverage made of A. keiskei juice,1 green grape juice, and pear juice may be a good drink to start with.

Whichever ingredients and consistency you choose, you should avoid drinks high in sugar because sugar contributes to dehydration.

Water

Water is the purest and most accessible option. Drinking water between alcoholic drinks and/or before you go to bed after a night of drinking alcohol will help you avoid dehydration and reduce the likelihood or severity of a hangover the next day. But if you’re reading this the day after drinking and didn’t take these preventive measures, drinking water while you have a hangover can still be helpful.

If you do not want to drink plain water, you can add fresh fruit or diluted fruit juice to flavor your water, aloe vera water, or coconut water.
