Explanation

Pain around both sides of the rib cage may be caused by various conditions. Common causes include:

Chest wall muscle strain or injury from physical activities, repetitive movements, or coughing

[Doctor's Note](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Inflammation of the cartilage connecting the ribs (costochondritis)

Nerve-related pain between the ribs (intercostal neuralgia)

Conditions affecting the lining of the lungs (pleurisy)

Less common causes include:

Twelfth rib syndrome, which causes pain in the lower rib area

Fibromyalgia, a chronic condition causing widespread musculoskeletal pain

Certain gastrointestinal conditions that can refer pain to the rib area
