What is a preop clearance exam?

Preop stands for pre-operation and refers to the period of time leading up to surgery. Therefore, a preop clearance exam is a comprehensive evaluation or checkup that you must receive before having surgery. It’s similar to a regular exam but with a few more tests and teaching moments.

The goal is to prepare you for your upcoming operation and give you an idea of what to expect. It’s also meant to prepare your surgeon and give them as much information as possible about your health. A preop clearance exam is standard procedure regardless of your type of surgery.

What should I expect during my preop?

A preop clearance exam is similar to any other physical exam, just a little more intense and comprehensive. The exam itself should last anywhere from one to two hours, depending on your upcoming operation. For minor procedures, however, the time frame could be less.

You should schedule your preop exam at least one month before your surgery, but it doesn’t have to be with your surgeon. Here are a few of the things you should expect during your appointment.

Medical history

One of the main things your preop exam will consist of is a comprehensive medical history. You’ll be asked a variety of questions about your current health, any known allergies, past medical conditions, or conditions that run in the family.

Physical exam

In addition to the medical history, you’ll also be given a comprehensive physical. You’ll have your blood pressure, respiration, heart rate, temperature, pulse ox, and possibly even blood work taken. If someone besides your surgeon performs your preop clearance exam, your information will be passed along to them and their team.
