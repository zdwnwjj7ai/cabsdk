Explanation

High stress seasons—whether driven by work deadlines, family obligations, or major life changes—can take a significant toll on your body. Chronic stress raises cortisol levels, disrupts sleep, and can leave you feeling drained. Increasing NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide) during these periods is one strategy many doctors recommend to help your cells cope, support energy production, and maintain overall well-being.

What Is NMN?

NMN is a naturally occurring molecule in all living cells. It serves as a direct precursor to NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), a critical coenzyme that:

Fuels mitochondrial energy production

Activates sirtuins (proteins involved in DNA repair and longevity)

Supports healthy immune and metabolic function

By boosting NMN levels, you're effectively giving your body the raw material it needs to keep NAD+ levels robust—especially important when stress accelerates cellular wear and tear.

How High Stress Affects NAD+ and Cellular Health

Increased Energy Demand

Stressful situations trigger "fight or flight," raising your metabolic rate.

Higher energy demand means faster NAD+ consumption in mitochondria.

Oxidative Stress & DNA Damage

Chronic stress elevates reactive oxygen species (ROS).

ROS damage DNA. Repairing DNA relies on NAD+-dependent enzymes (PARPs, sirtuins).

Inflammation & Immune Strain

Ongoing stress can keep inflammatory pathways active.

Immune cells need NAD+ to function optimally.

Without enough NAD+, cells struggle to meet energy needs, repair damage, and regulate inflammation—leading to fatigue, poor recovery, and mood dips.
