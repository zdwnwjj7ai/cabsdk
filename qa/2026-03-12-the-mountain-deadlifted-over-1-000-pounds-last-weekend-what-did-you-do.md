Quick update on Hafþór Júlíus Björnsson, a.k.a. , a.k.a. the guy who once threw a washing machine 15 feet through the air. When we last checked in on the Icelandic strongman, he was preparing for the 2018 Arnold Strongman Classic by deadlifting 970 pounds.

Well, the big man has topped himself, filming himself deadlifting more than 1,000 pounds in front of a whole host of screaming onlookers.
