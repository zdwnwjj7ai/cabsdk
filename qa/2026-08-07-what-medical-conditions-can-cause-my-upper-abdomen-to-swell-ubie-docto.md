1. Bloating:

Bloating is when your belly feels full and tight. It can make your abdomen look bigger. This can happen because of gas or other issues inside your abdomen. This has many different causes including but not limited to IBS, food intolerance, digestive issues.

2. Thyroid Problems:

Your thyroid is a gland in your neck that controls many things in your body, including how you digest food. When you have an underactive thyroid is can cause constipation and digestive issues which can lead to bloating.
