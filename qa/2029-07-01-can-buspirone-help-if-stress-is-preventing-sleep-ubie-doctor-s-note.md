Buspirone is generally not prescribed as a sleep aid for stress-related insomnia. Unlike sedatives or hypnotics, buspirone does not have well-established effects on sleep quality or duration. While it is FDA-approved to treat generalized anxiety disorder, its impact on sleep remains unclear and inconsistent in clinical research. If stress is disrupting your sleep, buspirone alone is unlikely to be the right solution.
