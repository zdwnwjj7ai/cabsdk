Does Keeps Work? Why Your Hair Is Thinning & Medically Approved Next Steps

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Keeps can help with male pattern baldness by offering FDA-approved finasteride and minoxidil, which slow hair loss and may regrow hair when started early and used consistently. However, results depend on the underlying cause and adherence to treatment. Keeps will not fix non-androgenic hair loss or restore long-dead follicles.

First: Why Is Your Hair Thinning?

Hair thinning in men is most commonly caused by male pattern baldness, also called androgenic alopecia. This condition affects up to 50% of men by age 50 and can start as early as the late teens or 20s.

What causes it?

Male pattern baldness is driven by:

Genetics (family history)

Hormones, specifically a byproduct of testosterone called DHT (dihydrotestosterone)

Increased sensitivity of hair follicles to DHT

Over time, DHT shrinks hair follicles. The hair becomes thinner, shorter, and lighter until the follicle eventually stops producing visible hair.

Are There Other Causes of Hair Thinning?

Yes. While androgenic alopecia is the most common cause, other medical conditions can contribute to hair loss:

Thyroid disorders

Iron deficiency

Autoimmune conditions (like alopecia areata)

Severe stress or illness

Rapid weight loss

Certain medications

If hair loss is sudden, patchy, accompanied by scalp pain, or paired with other symptoms (fatigue, weight changes, skin changes), it's important to speak with a doctor. Some underlying causes can be medically serious and require prompt treatment.

How Finasteride Works

Finasteride works by blocking the enzyme that converts testosterone into DHT. Lower DHT levels help:

Slow hair loss

Prevent further follicle miniaturization

Promote regrowth in some men

What the research shows:

About 80–90% of men see slowed hair loss.

Around 60–65% experience some regrowth.

It works best when started early.

Results typically appear after 3–6 months, with full evaluation at 12 months.

Finasteride must be taken consistently. If you stop, hair loss generally resumes within several months.

What Keeps Cannot Do

It's important to be realistic.

Keeps cannot:

Restore hair follicles that are completely dead

Provide instant results

Permanently cure genetic hair loss

Work without consistent use
