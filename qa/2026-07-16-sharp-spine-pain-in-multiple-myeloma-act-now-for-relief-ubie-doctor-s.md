Sharp spine pain in multiple myeloma is often caused by bone lesions, vertebral compression fractures, or nerve involvement. It can typically be managed through a combination of pain medications (such as analgesics or opioids), bisphosphonates to strengthen bone, radiation therapy, and procedures like vertebroplasty or kyphoplasty. Addressing the underlying myeloma with chemotherapy or targeted therapies also helps reduce pain and improve mobility and quality of life.

Managing sharp spine pain in patients with multiple myeloma involves a combination of approaches. Medications are often used to help control pain. These can include painkillers like acetaminophen, anti-inflammatory drugs, or stronger medications such as opioids if needed. Physical therapy can also be beneficial to improve movement and reduce pain.

In some cases, injections or other procedures may be recommended to help manage pain more directly.
