Explanation

Penile pain refers to any pain or discomfort experienced in the penis. According to the medical literature, this type of pain can be acute or chronic. Chronic penile pain is particularly challenging as it is not well-studied or managed, which can lead to difficulties in diagnosis and treatment.

Causes of Penile Pain:

Infections: Bacterial or fungal infections can cause inflammation and pain.

Injuries: Trauma or injury to the penis can result in pain.

Medical Conditions: Conditions like Peyronie's disease or urethritis may cause penile pain.
