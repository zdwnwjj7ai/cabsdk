Internal Medicine

Magnesium may help ease restless leg syndrome (RLS) symptoms, particularly in people with low magnesium levels. This essential mineral supports nerve and muscle function, which may explain its potential to calm the involuntary leg movements and uncomfortable sensations associated with RLS. However, current evidence is limited, and more research is needed to confirm how effective magnesium truly is as a treatment.

If you're experiencing restless legs, tingling, or sleep disturbances, it's worth understanding the root cause before starting any supplement. Symptoms like RLS can stem from iron deficiency, nerve issues, medication side effects, or other underlying conditions — each requiring a different approach. Explanation

Magnesium is a mineral that plays a role in muscle function and relaxation. Some studies suggest that taking magnesium supplements might help reduce the symptoms of restless leg syndrome (RLS), a condition that causes an uncontrollable urge to move the legs, usually due to uncomfortable sensations.

One study explored magnesium supplementation for RLS treatment and found potential benefits in easing symptoms.

Another research focused on magnesium therapy for periodic leg movements, a condition related to RLS, and reported positive outcomes.
