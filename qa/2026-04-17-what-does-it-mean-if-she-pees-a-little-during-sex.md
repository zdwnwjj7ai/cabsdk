My fiancée wants to combine phone plans and finances. Can I push back on joining accounts?

Absolutely, but don’t be weird about it. I believe in being 90 percent honest with your significant other. You’re a better partner when you’re happy as an individual, but technology is making it harder to live separate lives as a pair. When it comes to bank accounts, do not mess around. You always hear that communication about money is a big pain point for couples. Note that I didn’t just say “money”—it’s discussing it that makes everyone frantic. Talk your fiancée through how you want your accounts organized and why. Then keep talking. She’ll appreciate that more than you think.

He broke up with me because of “bad timing.” Legit excuse, or is he seeing someone else?

There’s a 50 percent chance he’s seeing someone else. There’s a 30 percent chance he’s really focused on work and can’t commit to dates right now. And there’s a 20 percent chance he realized he’s not that into it. Kidding! Who knows why people use the excuses they use to break up? It’s hard to be honest when you’re trying to spare someone’s feelings. The point is that you’re single now, so go find someone who thinks finding you was perfect timing. Don’t settle for less.

My new girlfriend had a threesome with her ex and another girl. What are the chances I’ll get one, too?

One threesome does not beget two. Following my math here? Let’s add up the signs: You have intel about one past threesome. That could have been the result of a pushy boyfriend, an unlikely encounter, a frisky friend, a planned strategy, a bad decision, or all of the above. Do you know if she enjoyed said threesome? Because that’s your biggest predictor of a repeat. With most topics, I would encourage you to come right out and ask her about it, but since it’s a new relationship, maybe ease into this one. Queue up Y Tu Mamá También (it has a famous threesome scene!) and see how she reacts. At the very least, you can use it as foreplay for two.
