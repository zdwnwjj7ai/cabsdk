# Seizure Warning: 7 Critical Health Issues You Must Identify Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

A seizure can be a sign of which underlying health problems?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Seizures can have many causes, including excessive alcohol consumption, brain trauma, structural central nervous system abnormalities, or oxygen deprivation from conditions like severe asthma. Other common triggers include high fever, low blood sugar, stroke, infections such as meningitis, and epilepsy. Identifying the underlying cause is essential for proper treatment and prevention of future episodes.

Explanation

Seizures can happen for different reasons. One reason is drinking too much alcohol or having alcohol dependency. People who drink a lot might have seizures because alcohol affects their brain.

Another reason for seizures can be problems in the brain, e.g. when the tissues in the brain are not normal. This can cause seizures and also make someone feel confused or see things that are not there.
