# Teosyal Non-Surgical Nose Contouring Training | Intermediate

Preview

About this lesson

Master the art of non-surgical nose reshaping with complete guidance from Dr. Juan Sopena in this live patient demonstration.

This lesson provides detailed instruction on correcting nasal asymmetry, improving frontonasal angles, and creating harmonious nasal contours using Teosyal® Ultimate from the Teoxane portfolio.

You'll learn critical safety protocols including nasal arterial anatomy, the essential pinch test for patient selection, and precise injection techniques for predictable, natural-looking results.

Ideal for aesthetic practitioners seeking to expand their non-surgical rhinoplasty skills with both needle and cannula approaches.

‍

Things you’ll learn

[Training Intermediate](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/0880760918.html)

How to assess nasal structure and identify septal deviation appearance, dorsal humps, and visible cartilage in frontal and profile views.

Critical nasal arterial anatomy and safe injection zones to minimize vascular complication risks.

The pinch test technique to assess patient candidacy and identify absolute contraindications including post-surgical noses.

Needle technique for frontonasal angle augmentation with perpendicular bone-contact injection.

[Non-Surgical Nose Contouring Training](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/6801009063.html)

Cannula technique to add soft tissue over the nasal bridge and smooth visible bone irregularities.

Strategic injection sequencing and proper molding techniques for symmetrical, natural results.

Learn non-surgical rhinoplasty directly from Dr. Juan Sopena using Teosyal® Ultimate with live patient demonstration and real-time narration.

Master both needle and cannula techniques with clear rationale for when each approach is indicated.

Understand critical vascular anatomy to minimize complication risks in nasal filler treatments.

[Contouring Training Intermediate](https://healingkinetics.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1338508826.html)

Perform nasal assessments and execute the pinch test to determine patient candidacy.

Apply detailed knowledge of nasal arterial anatomy to plan safe injection trajectories.

Perform precise needle injections in the frontonasal angle using bone-contact technique.

Create symmetrical, natural-looking nasal contours through strategic injection sequencing and molding.
