What is Hemophilia?

A hereditary bleeding disorder where blood cannot clot properly because of a low number of blood-clotting proteins (clotting factors), leading to prolonged bleeding even from minor injuries.

Treatment of Hemophilia

Hemophilia is usually treated by replacing the missing blood clotting factor through injections into the vein, allowing the blood to clot properly.
