What is Onychogryphosis?

This condition causes the affected nail to thicken, elongate, and curve with a yellow-brown color. It is more common in older adults. The cause is not clear.

Typical Symptoms of Onychogryphosis

Change in nail color

Change in nail shape

Skin problems

Diagnostic Questions for Onychogryphosis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Did your nail color change?

Did your nail shape change?

Do you have any skin problems

Treatment of Onychogryphosis

It can be left untreated. However, if it causes pain or discomfort, surgery is an option. Wearing well-fitting shoes and maintaining clean, short toenails can help prevent it from worsening.
