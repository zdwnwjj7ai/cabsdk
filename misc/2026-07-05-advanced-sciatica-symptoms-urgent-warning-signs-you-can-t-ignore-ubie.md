The final stages of sciatica are typically marked by severe leg pain, persistent numbness, and noticeable muscle weakness. These advanced symptoms often signal underlying spinal conditions such as spinal stenosis or a herniated disc compressing the sciatic nerve. Left untreated, late-stage sciatica can lead to loss of mobility, chronic nerve damage, or bladder and bowel dysfunction.

Explanation

Sciatica is a condition that affects the sciatic nerve, which runs from the lower back down the legs. In its advanced stages, symptoms can become more intense and may include severe pain that radiates from the lower back to the leg, as well as numbness or weakness in the leg muscles.

Potential Causes:

Spinal Stenosis: This is a narrowing of the spaces within the spine, which can put pressure on the nerves and is a known cause of sciatica.

Symptoms to Watch For:

Severe Pain: Pain may become more intense and persistent.

Numbness: There might be a loss of feeling in the leg.

Weakness: Muscle weakness in the leg can occur, making it hard to move.

It's important to seek medical advice if these symptoms are present, as they can impact daily activities and quality of life.
