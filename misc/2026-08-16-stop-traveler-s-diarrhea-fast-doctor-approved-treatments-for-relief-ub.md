Traveler's diarrhea is a common digestive illness that typically causes loose stools and stomach cramps after consuming contaminated food or water while traveling. In many cases, it resolves on its own within a few days without medical intervention. However, antibiotics such as azithromycin (Zithromax) may be prescribed to shorten the duration of symptoms or treat more severe cases.

Because symptoms of traveler's diarrhea can overlap with more serious conditions—like parasitic infections, food poisoning, or inflammatory bowel issues—it's important to understand what's actually driving your discomfort before deciding on next steps.
