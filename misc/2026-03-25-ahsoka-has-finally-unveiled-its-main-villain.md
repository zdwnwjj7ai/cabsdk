# 'Ahsoka' Has Finally Unveiled Its Main Villain

The following story contains light spoilers for Ahsoka Episode 6, "Far, Far, Away."

NOW THROUGH SIX of its eight episodes, and Ahsoka is finally starting to reveal its endgame. The series spent significant time in the earlygoing establishing a few things all at once; first and foremost, it had to establish the live-action versions of beloved animated characters like Ahsoka herself (Rosario Dawson), Sabine Wren (Natasha Liu Bordizzo), and Hera Syndulla (Mary Elizabeth Winstead). It also had to work to create its own standalone story with its own standalone problems: enter Ray Stevenson's Baylan Skoll and his apprentice, Shin Hati (Ivanna Sakhno), along with the sinister witch Morgan Elsbeth (Diana Lee Inosanto).

The show spent some time doing character stuff (puting Ahsoka in the World Between Worlds and having her spend time in various states of flashback with Anakin Skywalker), but now seems finally ready to set up its major hero vs villain conflict: Ahsoka versus Grand Admiral Thrawn.

Episode 6, "Far, Far, Away," largely put Ahsoka herself on the sidelines (outside of a brief cold open scene). Instead, the show finally introduced the villain its been building all along: Thrawn. Thrawn, with his blue skin and blood-red eyes, immediately proved himself to be a thrilling tactician with a cool head. He allied himself with his saviors, but made his ambition and ruthlessness clear to everyone in his orbit (and everyone watching at home).

And while we're seeing a bit of Baylan Skoll's rogue plan, it's clear that the show is setting us up for a major showdown before the end of the season between Thrawn and Ahsoka. Will Ahsoka's friends—who now include Ezra Bridger (Eman Esfandi) after he was found by Sabine—be able to help her out? Which side will the mercenaries Baylan and Shin ultimately take? And where does this story go from here?

That much remains to be seen in the show's final episodes. But until now, let's dive into that primary villain—and the man who makes Grand Admiral Thrawn so chilling.

After providing the voice for Grand Admiral Thrawn in 13 episodes of Star Wars Rebels, Lars Mikkelsen returns to the Star Wars world to play the character in live-action in Ahsoka. (If you've seen Rebels and still want to know more about this baddie, you can check out the many books that have also been written about him in the Star Wars canon.)

Mikkelsen—who revealed that it took as much as 3 hours to get into Thrawn's blue makeup—told Collider that he primarily enjoyed returning to play the villain because of how highly he regards the writing. "That’s what you want as an actor. You don’t want to reveal everything, you want your secrets and when it’s within the script, it’s the best," he said. "It’s effortless in that sense. You can only play the moment, and you don’t have to push anything because the story writing is so good that it will tell the story for you."]
