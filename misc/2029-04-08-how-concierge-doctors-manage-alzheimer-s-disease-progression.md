The stages of Alzheimer’s can be categorized as early, middle, and late.

Early-stage Alzheimer’s occurs when symptoms are mild and hard to notice. A person may occasionally forget some things, but they can still fully function on their own.

In middle-stage Alzheimer’s, symptoms have begun to worsen, and a person may be confused easily and have more erratic behavior. This stage is usually the longest and can last for years.

Late-stage Alzheimer’s is the most severe stage. By this point, a person is usually unable to care for themselves and may require full-time caretaking.

Patients with Alzheimer’s should seek out specialized care as soon as they notice symptoms like memory loss and difficulty completing everyday tasks.
