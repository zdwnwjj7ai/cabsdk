What is Benign Ovarian Tumor?

A non-cancerous growth of the ovaries. Follow-up is sometimes needed to ensure the tumors do not become cancerous.

Diagnostic Questions for Benign Ovarian Tumor

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Treatment of Benign Ovarian Tumor

Not all tumors require treatment. However, large or painful tumors may require surgery. Tumors that show signs of becoming cancerous also need to be removed.
