Medial meniscus tear – pain along the joint line

Explanation

When a patient marks the spot where their knee hurts on a diagram, it gives a clear picture of the pain's location. For example, if the pain is on the inner (medial) side of the knee, it might hint at conditions like osteoarthritis or a meniscal tear.

Research shows that different pain patterns can be linked with varying degrees of joint wear or injury. One study found that pain in specific areas is connected with how the knee feels and works. This means that a chart can help tie patient symptoms to common knee problems seen in osteoarthritis or tears.

Additionally, these charts are useful because they are simple to use and non-invasive, helping doctors decide if they need to recommend more tests like X-rays or an MRI.
