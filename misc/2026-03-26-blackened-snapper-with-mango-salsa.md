# Blackened Snapper With Mango Salsa

USE 2 snapper, catfish, or tilapia fillets, 4–5 oz each

PLUS 1 mango (skin and seed removed), cut into cubes

PLUS 1 ripe avocado (skin and pit removed), cut into cubes

PLUS Juice of one lime

PLUS 1 Tbsp blackening spices (we like Zatarain's)

[Blackened Snapper With](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/)

YOU'LL ALSO NEED

1/4 c minced onion

1/2 c chopped cilantro

[Snapper With Mango](https://benpepsi.com/blog/0738765580.html)

[Snapper With Mango Salsa](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/7163920379.html)

Salt and pepper

Vegetable oil

[Blackened Snapper With Mango](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

HOW TO MAKE IT

1. Combine the mango, avocado, lime juice, onion, and cilantro in a mixing bowl. Season with salt and pepper.

2. Preheat a large sauté or grill pan over high heat and add enough oil to coat it.

[Blackened Snapper With](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/blog/1727935788.html)

3. Rub the fish with enough of the blackening spice to thoroughly cover each side. When the oil is hot (almost smoking), carefully add the fillets to the pan and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until a nice, dark crust has developed. The fish is done when it flakes easily with light pressure from your finger.

4. Top each fillet with the mango salsa.

EAT WITH: Brown rice and black beans

Per Serving: 430 calories, 40 grams (g) protein, 45 g carbohydrates, 8 g fat (2 g saturated), 7 g fiber, 590 milligrams sodium
