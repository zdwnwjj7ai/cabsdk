What is a School Physical Examination?

A school physical exam is a routine checkup for children entering certain schools or playing school sports. These exams assess a student’s overall health, check for signs of potential medical issues, and ensure that all necessary immunizations have been administered.

During a school physical, your child’s doctor will assess their overall physical health, measuring height and weight and checking their blood pressure and heart rate. The doctor may also check their eyesight, ears, mouth, throat, lungs, heart, abdomen, and lymph nodes. Depending on your child’s age and health history, the doctor may perform additional tests or screenings to look for potential issues.

It’s important to note that some states require paperwork to be completed by the doctor for the physical exam to be considered valid. Make sure to ask your child’s doctor if they need any documentation signed or filled out before submitting the physical exam to the school.

Additionally, remember that school physicals are different from sports physicals, so double-check what type of physical your child’s school requires.

Why are School Physicals Important?

School physical examinations provide a comprehensive overview of your child’s health, allowing for detecting any possible issues or diseases before they become more serious. The exam also allows for the verification of immunizations and vaccines and provides a chance to catch up on anything that may be missing. The exams also allow your doctor to check for any issues that may be present and monitor your child’s growth and development. Finally, school physicals offer an opportunity to monitor developmental progress and answer any questions you may have about your child’s health.

School physicals are a great way to have your child’s health questions answered in one visit. With regular exams, your child can stay healthy and be ready for their school year activities.

Where can I Get a School Physical Exam?

Most parents visit a doctor’s office or clinic for a school physical exam. During a clinic visit, your child will be seen by a qualified healthcare provider, such as a doctor or nurse practitioner.
