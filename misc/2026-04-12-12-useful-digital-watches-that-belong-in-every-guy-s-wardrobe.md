Should I Buy a Digital Watch?

Like mentioned earlier, digital watches are kind of overlooked these days. The watch category isn't really define as being "smart" like a smartwatch, yet you still get a level of features on a digital watch that are useful and more advanced than an analog watch.

AFFORDABILITY

[Useful Digital Watches That](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

The biggest thing digital watches have going for them is they are mostly affordable. Unless you're buying a specialty digital watch or a digital watch from a luxury brand, you can expect to spend around $100 for a quality digital watch (and sometimes much less).

FUN TO STYLE

Due to the fact digital watches have been around for decades now, a lot of them have swung back around in the fashion world and carry a retro-look that many people like these days. There are also digital watches that are more novelty and nostalgic, pointing back to decades like the 1970s and 1980s with quirky futuristic designs that are fun to show off to friends.

SIMPLE DISPLAY

Digital watch displays are typically large and come with backlight functions, making them much easier to read at times over an analog watch. Digital watch also have simple readings, unlike some popular smartwatches where the watch face can look too cluttered or feel too bright.
