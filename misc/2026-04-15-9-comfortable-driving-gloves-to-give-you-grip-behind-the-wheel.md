When was the last time you saw anyone wearing driving gloves? It was likely watching an F1 race, to which we say, why let them have all the fun? Just because you're not driving a $14 million speed machine doesn't mean you can't drive in style.

Driving gloves originated in the late 1890s when all steering wheels were made from uncovered metal or wood. Drivers needed to wear gloves in order to properly handle the wheel in extreme temperatures, as well as protect them from splinters. Early race cars had steering wheels taken directly from road cars with mostly wood steering wheels. In modern times, classic car owners wear driving gloves in order to protect the vintage wooden or ivory steering wheel.

Driving gloves are used for a few main reasons: grip, protection, and of course, style. Unless your dream job involves being a stunt driver for the Fast and the Furious franchise, chances are you don't need driving gloves. But if you want to prevent wear and tear on a leather steering wheel and look good doing it, treat yourself to a pair of driving gloves.

From vintage-inspired to luxury leather, we've rounded up the best driving gloves to get you riding in style.
