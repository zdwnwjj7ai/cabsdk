What Is Regenerative Joint Pain Therapy?

Regenerative therapies focus on repairing and rejuvenating damaged tissue rather than masking symptoms. By activating the body’s own healing mechanisms, these treatments offer a powerful alternative to pain medications and surgery.

At MD Esthetics, we tailor treatment using one or more of the following:

Prolotherapy – Involves targeted injections of a natural irritant to stimulate healing and collagen production in injured ligaments and joints.

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) – Uses growth factor-rich plasma from your own blood to accelerate tissue repair and reduce inflammation.
