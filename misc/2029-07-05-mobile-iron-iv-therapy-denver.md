Iron deficiency can be diagnosed with blood tests that measure:

Ferritin: A blood protein that contains iron and can help your doctor understand how much iron your body stores.

Hemoglobin (HB): A blood protein that helps transport oxygen throughout your body.

Complete blood count (CBC): A panel that measures your current levels of red blood cells, white blood cells, hemoglobin, platelets, and hematocrit. A low blood count can indicate iron deficiency anemia.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Venofer Iron IV, and how does it work?

Venofer Iron IV is an intravenous iron replacement therapy. It delivers iron directly into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system, and is known for its rapid absorption to help to replenish iron levels efficiently.

Who is a candidate for Venofer Iron IV?

Venofer Iron IV is typically recommended for individuals diagnosed with iron deficiency anemia or those with difficulty tolerating or responding to oral iron supplements.

What are the benefits of choosing Venofer Iron IV over oral iron supplements?

Venofer Iron IV offers faster and more effective absorption compared to oral supplements. It is particularly beneficial for individuals with absorption issues or those who experience side effects from oral iron.

Is Venofer Iron IV painful?

The administration of Venofer Iron IV is generally well-tolerated. While some individuals may experience minor discomfort at the injection site, the process is relatively quick, and healthcare providers take measures to minimize any discomfort.

How long does a Venofer Iron IV session take?

The duration of a Venofer Iron IV session can vary, but it typically takes about 60-90 minutes.

Are there any side effects associated with Venofer Iron IV?

Like any medical treatment, Venofer Iron IV may have side effects. These can include nausea, headache, or dizziness. Serious side effects are rare but can be discussed with our healthcare provider.

Can I receive Venofer Iron IV at home?

Yes, in-home administration of Venofer Iron IV is available for eligible individuals. This option provides convenience and comfort while ensuring you receive the necessary iron replacement therapy to help you feel your best.

How long does it take to see the benefits of Venofer Iron IV?

Individual responses vary, but many people report increased energy levels and improvement in symptoms within a few weeks of starting Venofer Iron IV treatment.

What tests are used to diagnose an iron deficiency?

CBC, CMP, Iron, TIBC, and Ferritin
