What is Acquired Hemophilia?

Acquired hemophilia is a rare autoimmune disorder causing bleeding tendencies due to the development of antibodies against clotting factors.

Typical Symptoms of Acquired Hemophilia

Purple marks or spots on the skin

Black, shiny stool

Blood in stool

Bleeding and bruising easily

History of connective tissue, autoimmune or rheumatic disease

Nosebleed

Blood in urine

Producing phlegm that contains blood

Diagnostic Questions for Acquired Hemophilia

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you noticed any purple marks or spots on your skin?

Have you had black, shiny stools?

Have you noticed any blood in your stool?

Do you bruise or bleed easily?

Have you ever been diagnosed with a connective tissue, autoimmune, or rheumatic disease?

Treatment of Acquired Hemophilia

Treatment focuses on managing bleeding episodes and addressing the underlying cause. Treatment options may include immune therapy to suppress the abnormal antibodies, desmopressin for mild cases, and factor replacement therapies.
