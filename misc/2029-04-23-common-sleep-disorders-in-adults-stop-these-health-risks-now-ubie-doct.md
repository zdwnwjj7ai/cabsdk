Common sleep disorders in adults include insomnia, sleep apnea, restless legs syndrome (RLS), and narcolepsy. Insomnia involves difficulty falling or staying asleep, while sleep apnea causes breathing interruptions during sleep. RLS triggers uncomfortable leg sensations, and narcolepsy leads to sudden daytime sleep episodes. Each condition can significantly impact sleep quality, energy levels, mental focus, and long-term health.

Explanation

Sleep disorders can make it hard for adults to get a good night's sleep. Here are some common types:

[Ubie Doctor's](https://tiancansz.com/0tjxnsp3y/)

Insomnia: This is when you have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep. It can make you feel tired during the day.

Sleep Apnea: This happens when your breathing stops and starts while you sleep. It can make you feel very sleepy during the day.

[Health Risks](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/)

Restless Legs Syndrome (RLS): This is a strong urge to move your legs, often with strange feelings in your legs. It can make it hard to fall asleep.

Narcolepsy: This is when you suddenly feel very sleepy during the day and might fall asleep suddenly.

These sleep disorders can affect how you feel during the day and your health. If you're experiencing any of these symptoms, try our free symptom checker to better understand what might be causing your sleep issues, and talk to a doctor about your concerns.
