# Watch This Guy Run a Full Marathon in a Pair of Jeans

Runner and YouTuber Elijah Orr is always looking for a new running challenge. Most recently, he ate and trained like Kenyan marathoner Eliud Kipchoge for 24 hours, challenged himself to take 100,000 steps in one day, did the notorious CrossFit Murph workout, and ran a full marathon around his house during quarantine.

His most recent stunt is a little more uncomfortable than the others: running in a pair of jeans. The Blue Jean Mile record was recently broken by runner Johnny Gregorek, who clocked in at 4:06.25. But for Orr, he's taking it to another level: he plans to run a Blue Jean Marathon—that's a full marathon 26.2 miles—while rocking denim.

He fuels up for the run with black coffee and an English muffin smothered in jam and peanut butter, then makes the drive to his chosen course.

He's joined by his friend Pat (who skips out on the jeans) for the run. By mile 4, the chafing hit Orr hard. His videographer, Tyler, interrupts the video to announce he's heading back to the car to grab his nut butter so that way he doesn't rash up. "Hopefully I can get back to him soon enough," Tyler says. "We don't want Elijah's nuts to be chafed."

Orr receives the aid in time, then lubes up to get back to the run. At mile 16, he stops for a bathroom break and discusses how it's going.

"Going decent. Ten miles to go from here" says Orr. "You gotta just go for it and embrace the pain. Just don't even think about, click off the miles. Just grinding it out,"

But by mile 21, the sweat has started to soak through the jeans, and they're starting to fall off his waist.

"They're sagging. I got the wrong size. I should've gotten the 27 waist instead of the 28," he says.

Orr keeps going, pulling up his jeans every few strides. And then, a miracle occurs: Pat spots a string on the side of the trail that Orr uses as a makeshift belt to keep his jeans up.
