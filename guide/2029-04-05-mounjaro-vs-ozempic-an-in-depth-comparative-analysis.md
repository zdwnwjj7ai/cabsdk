Use in Unique Patient Groups

When prescribing medications like Mounjaro and Ozempic, it’s important to consider how they affect different groups of patients. This includes children, the elderly, pregnant women, and those with other health conditions.

For children, the safety and effectiveness of Mounjaro and Ozempic haven’t been well-studied. Therefore, their use in pediatric populations may be limited or require careful monitoring by professionals.

Mounjaro is only currently approved for use in adults; however, Eli Lily (the manufacturer of Mounjaro) is in the process of starting clinical trials for children between the ages of 6 and 11. Ozempic is approved for use in adults and children over the age of 12.

Elderly people might react differently to medications because their metabolism and organ function change with age. This means they might need different doses or more frequent check-ups to make sure the medicine works safely and effectively.

Neither Ozempic nor Mounjaro are recommended for use during pregnancy. There have not been enough studies conducted to approve their use for pregnant women.
